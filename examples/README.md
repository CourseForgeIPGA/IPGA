# Example: "Product Photos That Sell" (fictional)

**Pitch:** I help small apparel brands go from phone snapshots to scroll-stopping product photos in 4 weeks, without a studio.

**Outcome:** Learners can shoot, edit and publish a consistent product photo set for 10 items.

| Module | Milestone | Sample lessons |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Quick Win | One clean product shot today | Window-light setup; Phone camera settings; Your first flat lay |
| 2. Lighting | Consistent light on any budget | Diffusion; Reflectors; Cheap LED setups |
| 3. Styling | Shots that match your brand | Mood boards; Props; On-body vs flat lay |
| 4. Editing & Publishing | A finished catalog | Batch editing; Backgrounds; Exporting for shop + socials |

**Funnel:** Free "5-minute window-light checklist" → 7-email sequence → sales page → course + community.
