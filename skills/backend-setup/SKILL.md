# Back-End Setup

## Step 1 - Pricing
Ask: audience budget, outcome value, competitor prices, support level.
Suggest tiers:
- Self-paced (course only)
- Course + community
- Course + community + live calls/coaching
Explain the reasoning; the expert decides.

## Step 2 - Platform
Help choose based on needs (all-in-one vs separate tools). Common options: Teachable, Thinkific, Kajabi, Podia, Skool, Circle, or self-hosted. Ask about budget, transaction fees, community needs, and branding control. Verify current pricing on the vendor site before recommending.

## Step 3 - Sales funnel
Fill `templates/sales-funnel.md`:
1. Lead magnet (free checklist, mini-course, or webinar) tied to Module 1's quick win
2. Opt-in page
3. Welcome + nurture email sequence (5-7 emails): story, problem, quick win, proof, objection handling, offer, deadline
4. Sales page: headline, problem, transformation, curriculum, proof, pricing, FAQ, guarantee
5. Checkout + onboarding email

Draft the copy. Never invent testimonials or results - use placeholders until the expert supplies real ones.

## Step 4 - Community hosting
Fill `templates/community-setup.md`: platform, channels, welcome flow, weekly rituals, moderation rules, engagement prompts.

## Step 5 - Launch
Work through `templates/launch-checklist.md`: beta cohort, feedback loop, launch window, post-launch review.
