# Curriculum Design

Goal: take a vague "I know a lot about X" and produce a clear vision brief and a curriculum the expert can record.

## Step 1 - Streamline the vision
Interview the expert. Ask one question at a time, max 6 total:
1. What do you teach, in one sentence?
2. Who exactly is it for? (job, skill level, situation)
3. What can they DO after the course that they can't do now? (the transformation)
4. What's the biggest mistake beginners in this area make?
5. What results have you or your clients gotten?
6. Format preference: self-paced video, cohort, workshop, or hybrid?

Write the answers into `templates/vision.md` format. Push back if the audience is "everyone" or the outcome is vague - narrow it to one learner and one measurable result.

## Step 2 - Work backward from the outcome
- Write the final outcome as a single observable skill.
- List the 3-7 milestones a learner must hit to reach it. Each milestone = one module.
- For each module, list 3-6 lessons. Each lesson teaches ONE thing and ends with an action.
- Cut anything that doesn't serve the outcome. Put it in a "bonus" list instead.

## Step 3 - Build the curriculum
Fill `templates/curriculum.md`. Every lesson needs:
- Title (benefit-led)
- Learning objective ("By the end, you can...")
- Format (talking head, screen share, worksheet, live call)
- Action/assignment
- Estimated length (aim 5-15 min)

## Step 4 - Sanity check
- Is there a quick win in Module 1 (within the first hour)?
- Can a learner finish in a realistic time for the audience?
- Does each module end with a checkpoint or project?

Output: a completed vision brief and curriculum. Then suggest moving to the `course-production` skill.
