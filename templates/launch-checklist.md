# Launch Checklist

## Beta (optional, recommended)
- [ ] 5-20 beta students at a discount
- [ ] Feedback form after each module
- [ ] Collect real testimonials (with permission)

## Pre-launch (2-4 weeks)
- [ ] Lead magnet live
- [ ] Email sequence scheduled
- [ ] Sales page live, checkout tested
- [ ] Community ready, welcome flow tested
- [ ] Content plan for social posts

## Launch week
- [ ] Open cart
- [ ] Live session / webinar
- [ ] Daily emails
- [ ] Close cart (if using a deadline)

## Post-launch
- [ ] Review numbers: opt-ins, conversion, refunds
- [ ] Student feedback review
- [ ] Update curriculum
